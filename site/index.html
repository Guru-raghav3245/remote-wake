Three steps, about five minutes

Each app walks you through it and ticks every step off for you.

1

Set up your Mac

Install Remote Wake, connect Tailscale (a free account) and turn on "Stay awake on AC". One password prompt.

2

Install the phone app

Sideload the Android app and sign in to Tailscale with the same account.

3

Tap your Mac

Your Mac shows up in the list. Type your user name and you are in a real terminal.

Made for leaving your Mac behind

Awake with the lid closed

A small background service keeps the Mac running while it is plugged in. On battery it sleeps normally.

Private by design

Your phone and Mac talk directly over Tailscale. There is no Remote Wake account and no server of ours in the middle.

A real terminal

Full-screen programs, an extra keys row for Esc, Tab and Ctrl, and automatic reconnect when the network drops.

Questions

Does my Mac stay awake on battery?

No. It stays awake only while plugged in. On battery it sleeps like normal, which protects your battery.

What is Tailscale and do I need an account?

Tailscale makes the private link between your devices. You need a free account, and you sign in once on the Mac and once on the phone with the same account.

Every so often my phone asks me to approve a connection.

That is Tailscale's safety check, about every 12 hours. The app shows an Approve button that opens the approval page.

What if the connection drops?

The app reconnects by itself, but programs that were running in the terminal stop. Run long jobs inside tmux on the Mac to keep them alive.

What happens after a power cut?

If FileVault is on, the Mac waits for its password at startup, and nothing can reach it until someone unlocks it.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet.
